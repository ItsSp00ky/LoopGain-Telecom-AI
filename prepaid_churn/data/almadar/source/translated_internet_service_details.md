*   **What is the "Net fi Waqtuh" service?**
    The "Net fi Waqtuh" service, or what is known as "Emergency Net," is a service dedicated to prepaid subscribers, allowing them to access the internet even if they don't have enough balance. This service is especially useful when the subscriber cannot recharge their balance at the current moment to subscribe to one of the available packages. It should be noted that the subscription value for the service is deducted and recovered from the balance at the first recharge operation performed by the subscriber.

*   **How to subscribe to the "Net fi Waqtuh" service?**
    Subscribers can easily subscribe to the "Net fi Waqtuh" service via the quick commands menu by dialing *000#. It is also possible to subscribe through the Almadar application available on smartphones, which provides a convenient and fast way to activate the service at any time.

*   **Service Features:**

# 64801.png

1.  This service is available to all prepaid subscribers without exception, making it accessible to everyone who needs an emergency internet connection.
2.  The subscriber can subscribe to the service again in case they have returned the debt value for the previous subscription, allowing repeated use of the service when needed.
3.  The subscription value for the service is automatically recovered at the first recharge operation or when transferring balance to the subscriber's account, ensuring that the subscriber does not lose the subscription value.
4.  It is not possible to subscribe to the service if the subscriber's balance is negative, i.e., in case of subscribing to the "Rasid fi Waqtuh" service, to ensure there is no conflict between the two services.
5.  To benefit from this service, the subscriber's current balance must be one dinar or less at the time of subscription, in addition to the remaining internet quota in the basic package being less than 250 megabytes.
6.  It is possible to subscribe to other packages such as the "Macchiato" or "Off-Peak" packages, etc., even if the subscriber has activated the "Net fi Waqtuh" service, which gives greater flexibility in choosing suitable packages.

7.  The subscriber can easily monitor the remaining internet quota volume directly through the application, or by using the main services menu by dialing *000#, providing an easy way to monitor your consumption.
8.  The internet quota value provided by the service is 2 gigabytes, an appropriate amount to meet the subscriber's emergency internet needs.
9.  The service validity period is only three days from the moment of subscription, meaning that the available quota must be used within this period.
10. The subscription value for the "Net fi Waqtuh" service is five dinars, a reasonable cost for obtaining an emergency internet connection in case the balance runs out.
