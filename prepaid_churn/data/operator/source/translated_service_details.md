It is a distinguished service provided by the company to subscribers who face difficulty in recharging their balance at the current time. This service allows users to obtain an immediate additional balance when their basic balance runs out, enabling them to continue using the company's services without interruption. Subscribers can benefit from this additional balance across various types of services provided by the company, whether local or international calls, sending short text messages, or even subscribing to various internet services. This service aims to ensure the continuity of connection and communication for subscribers at any time without the need to wait until they can recharge their balance, which enhances user convenience and provides them with a smoother and more flexible user experience.

Provided that the value of the additional balance is recovered and deducted at the first recharge operation.

You can subscribe to the service via the smart menu (*140#) or directly via the short code (\*140\*5#).

**Features:**
* 1. You can subscribe via the menu (*140#) or the short code (\*140\*5#).
* 2. It is required that the basic balance is ≤ 0.5 dinars to subscribe.
* 3. The service is available in three denominations (5, 3, 1) dinars according to the subscriber's consumption.
* 4. To inquire about the balance and debt, use (*111#) or (*140#) and follow the instructions.
* 5. The additional balance is used for all company services (calls, messages, internet).
* 6. The additional balance is added to the basic balance, and the total balance appears along with the due debt upon inquiry.
* 7. It is not possible to subscribe again except after recharging the balance and paying the debt.
* 8. You can subscribe multiple times a day after recharging the balance and paying the debt.
* 9. When recharging a balance ≥ the due debt, the debt is deducted and the subscription is canceled immediately.
* 10. After 48 hours, if the basic balance is greater than the debt, the debt is deducted and the subscription is canceled.
