# T12 sequence benchmark

Generated by `uv run churn sequence-benchmark` (ticket T12).

A Keras LSTM (32 units, dropout 0.2, Adam, batch 256) reads the two monthly steps of each window as a sequence of 53 measures plus tenure.
It is trained on the same training customers, stopped on the same validation customers, calibrated the same way, and scored once on the same frozen test window as T7.
No T7 choice was changed, and the champion was not retrained.

Seed 42 through `keras.utils.set_random_seed`, on the torch backend; the same machine reproduces this table, and another one can differ in the last digits.

## Would this model be released?

The four success thresholds of decision 13, applied to the LSTM:

| Check | Value | Required | Passed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of churners among the riskiest 10% of customers | 0.4941 | >= 0.5 | no |
| PR-AUC divided by the churn rate | 5.3462 | >= 3.0 | yes |
| PR-AUC against the logistic regression baseline | 0.2326 | > 0.2770 | no |
| Distance between mean predicted and observed churn rate | 0.0016 | <= 0.01 | yes |

**No**: it fails 2 of the four, so it would not be released (decision 13).

## Verdict

LightGBM wins, as expected: PR-AUC 0.3477 against the LSTM's 0.2326, 33.1% lower. With two monthly steps the movement between them is one subtraction, the T5 features hand it to LightGBM directly, and the LSTM has to learn it from 2 timesteps and a small number of churners. The recurrent architecture has nothing to remember here, so it is the wrong tool for this data shape, not a badly tuned one. It also lands below the logistic regression baseline (0.2770), which is the check a new model has to clear before anyone discusses architecture.

## What this does not settle

- Two monthly steps are not a sequence. With six or twelve months per customer the comparison would be worth running again, and the recurrent model would have something to remember.
- The upGrad export has one row per customer per month, so nothing finer than a month can be modelled here. Call-detail records would change that, and they are not in this data.
- The frozen champion stays the champion whatever this table says: the test window was spent once in T7, and choosing a model on it now would be the mistake decision 6 exists to prevent.
