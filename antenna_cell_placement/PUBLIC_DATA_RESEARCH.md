# Public data path while operator evidence is unavailable

Reviewed 2026-09-26. This is a source-selection record for real, public observations and official statistics. Operator asset verification, independent site surveys, and engineer-approved RF thresholds remain pending. No generic equipment parameters or synthetic signal measurements are substituted for them.

| Source | Public evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Libya Bureau of Statistics and Census, 2022 regional population](https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/groups/population_number) | 44 observed table rows: male and female counts for 22 named regions. Portal marks it “Other (Open).” | **Use for review.** The pinned CSV checks the 2020 WorldPop demand layer at matching administrative areas. It is not a 2026 population surface. |
| [Libya General Authority for Communications and Informatics, mobile users by technology, 2019–2025](https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/comm) | Seven annual national rows for 2G/3G and LTE/4G. Portal marks it “Other (Open).” | **Use for context.** The table gives a real technology trend, but not local traffic, coverage, unique subscribers, or RF configuration. |
| [FAO mobile broadband coverage](https://www.fao.org/agroinformatics/training-and-resources/data-sets/data-set-detail/data-set-1/en) and the [ITU metadata for underlying operator submissions](https://bbmaps.itu.int/geonetwork/srv/api/records/2741dffc-4c7f-4261-9c02-eb54f01f5165) | Raster compiled from operator submissions and OpenCellID. The ITU metadata says the raw coverage data is restricted to research use and cannot be redistributed; submissions are incomplete and signal-class conventions vary. | **Hold.** Confirm the exact Libya raster release, license, operator participation, and its overlap with our OpenCellID source before a local comparison. It cannot establish current, operator-specific coverage by itself. |
| [M-Lab open measurements](https://www.measurementlab.net/data/) | Public CC0 Internet performance tests. | **Hold.** These test network throughput rather than sector RF signal. Access and spatial/operator support in Libya require a separate gate. |
| [Public ArcGIS Libya tower layer](https://services8.arcgis.com/oTalEaSXAuyNT7xf/arcgis/rest/services/libya_Telecom/FeatureServer/0) | Point layer with OpenCellID fields such as MCC, MNC, LAC, cell ID, samples, and timestamps. | **Do not count as independent site confirmation.** It may duplicate the already ingested OpenCellID observations. |

The adopted CSVs and their exact upstream URLs, source dates, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes are in [the manifest](data/external/libya_official_2022_2025/manifest.json). `public-evidence-evaluate` compares all 22 official region names to the project's 22 administrative boundaries; 19 have a direct match after Arabic spelling normalization. It leaves `الواحات`, `المنطقة الغربية`, and `وادي الحياة` unmatched instead of guessing a boundary. In those 19 regions, the 2020 WorldPop pixel-count total is 97.37% of the official 2022 count. The years and methods differ, so this is a consistency check, not a measured error rate or an automatic correction. The national LTE/4G share of the published 2025 total is 32.11%; it does not imply local LTE service.

Next public-data experiment: evaluate the existing phone measurements against fixed geographic and temporal holdouts for operator-specific measured-service support. Only areas with explicit metric type and adequate samples should inform a descriptive weak-service layer. Keep the national placement score unchanged until that layer beats a pre-registered baseline on untouched evidence. An RF prediction still needs accepted engineering inputs.
